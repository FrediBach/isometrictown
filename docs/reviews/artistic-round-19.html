Artistic changes — round 19

15 November 2026 · Original review

Broad-pen coordinate labels around the frame now have room to read. Their cap height grows with the nib, and fewer coordinates are printed. The geographic interval remains a whole number of seconds or minutes. Margins too small for readable coordinates keep the border without coordinate ticks or labels.

Frame lettering at 0.7 mm

The footer moves down to clear enlarged labels beneath the frame. Its title, scale bar, compass and imprint retain their existing sizing rules; tiny footer lettering still needs a separate broad-pen treatment. This change addresses the perimeter coordinates only.

Verification and limits

Reviewed the broad monochrome A4 golden, before-and-after Mollis sheets at 1:1000 and 1:2000 with a 0.7 mm pen, their busiest close-ups, and enlarged frame labels. Only the broad-pen golden changed. All scene feature and facet totals, counts and census remain identical; the other eleven goldens remain identical. The broad golden estimate falls from 28.1 to 27.5 minutes because fewer labels require fewer pen lifts.

The LOD lab excludes marginalia from feature scoring, so its unchanged scene scores do not measure the lettering improvement. Tests check landscape and portrait sheets, readable cap height, fewer labels, sheet-edge clearance and omission in narrow margins. Build and all 595 tests pass.
