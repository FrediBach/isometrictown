    About Isometric Town

    Isometric Town draws any town in isometric 3D as clean linework for pen plotters. Pick a place, frame it, and download an SVG with one layer per pen, ready for an AxiDraw, Inkscape or vpype.

    Buildings, streets, water and parks come from OpenStreetMap. Building heights come from the map data where it has them. Roof shapes, trees, cars and lamps are made up, with a seed so the same settings always give the same drawing.

    Hidden lines are removed before you download, so nothing is drawn twice and nothing shows through a wall.
